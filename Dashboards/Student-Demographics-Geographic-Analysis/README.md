# Student Demographics & Geographic Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes student demographic and geographic data for Ballotté Dance Academy #Puchong, a small dance academy in Puchong, Selangor.

The analysis was conducted to help the business better understand its existing student base, identify where students are located, measure the academy's customer catchment area, and identify potential areas for future student acquisition.

The data was cleaned and prepared using Python before being analyzed and visualized in Tableau. Additional geographic analysis was conducted using student locations and distance from the academy.

## Tools Used

- Python (Pandas) – Data cleaning, validation, and geographic data preparation
- Tableau – Data analysis, visualization, and dashboard development
- Google Colab – Python development environment
- Geographic Mapping – Student location and customer catchment analysis

## Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer:

- What are the main age groups within the current student population?
- Which areas and townships have the highest concentration of students?
- How are students geographically distributed around the academy?
- How far are students travelling to attend the academy?
- Which residential areas represent the academy's strongest customer catchments?
- Which surrounding residential areas could be potential target markets for future student acquisition?
- What is the citizenship composition of the current student population?

## Key Insights

- The dataset contains 99 students across 28 identified areas, with an average student age of 7.6 years.
- Students aged 4–6 represent the largest age group with 53 students, followed by ages 7–9 with 22 students.
- Puchong is the main student location with 69 students, while Cyberjaya contributes 21 students.
- Cyberjaya is the largest individual area concentration with 21 students, followed by Bandar Bukit Puchong with 13 and Taman Putra Prima with 9.
- Malaysian students represent 85.86% of the current student population.
- Among the 68 students represented in the top 10 areas, 47 live within approximately 5 km of the academy.
- The remaining 21 students in the top 10 areas are from Cyberjaya, approximately 5–10 km from the academy.
- The geographic distribution shows a strong customer corridor extending south and southeast from the academy toward Bandar Bukit Puchong, Putra Prima, Putra Perdana, 16 Sierra, and Cyberjaya.
- Cyberjaya represents a significant secondary catchment despite being farther from the academy, indicating that some families are willing to travel beyond the immediate Puchong area.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize student acquisition in Puchong and Cyberjaya, where the academy already has its strongest customer base.
- Target specific Cyberjaya residential communities where existing students are concentrated.
- Use referral campaigns in residential communities with existing students to reach nearby families.
- Focus marketing and programmes on children aged 4–9, the academy's largest student segment.
- Target residential developments along the existing Puchong South–Cyberjaya customer corridor.
- Test marketing in nearby residential areas similar to the academy's existing high-performing student locations.
- Investigate why Cyberjaya families are willing to travel farther to the Puchong studio and use these factors in future marketing.
- Assess additional demand in Cyberjaya before considering future classes, transport options, or expansion.
