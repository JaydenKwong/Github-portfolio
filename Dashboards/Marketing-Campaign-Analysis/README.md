# Digital Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes digital advertising campaign performance to identify which marketing strategies generate the strongest advertising returns.

The data was cleaned and prepared using Python before being visualized in Tableau. An interactive dashboard was created to analyze overall campaign performance across advertising platforms, campaign objectives, creative formats, time, and retargeting strategies.

## Tools Used

- Python (Pandas) – Data cleaning and preparation
- Tableau – Data visualization and interactive dashboard
- Google Colab – Python development environment

## Key KPIs

- Total Ad Spend: $43.5M
- Total Revenue: $284.2M
- Total Profit: $240.7M
- Overall ROAS: 6.54x
- Conversion Rate: 4.30%
- Cost Per Acquisition (CPA): $66.27

## Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer:

- Which advertising platforms generate the highest ROAS?
- Which campaign objectives produce the strongest returns?
- How does advertising performance change over time?
- Which creative formats perform best?
- How does retargeting performance compare with non-retargeting campaigns?

## Dashboard

![Digital Marketing Performance Dashboard](Digital%20Marketing%20Performance%20Dashboard.png)

The interactive dashboard allows users to filter campaign performance by platform, campaign objective, date, creative format, and retargeting strategy.

## Key Insights

- TikTok generated the highest ROAS at 11.18x, followed by Facebook at 10.39x.
- Conversion campaigns achieved the highest ROAS among campaign objectives at 8.01x.
- Video was the strongest-performing creative format with a ROAS of 7.27x.
- Retargeting campaigns generated 9.50x ROAS compared with 5.56x for non-retargeting campaigns.
- ROAS varied considerably over time, showing periods of both stronger and weaker advertising performance.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize advertising budget toward TikTok and Facebook, as they generated the highest ROAS among the platforms analyzed.
- Increase the use of conversion-focused campaigns when the objective is to maximize direct advertising returns.
- Prioritize video creatives while continuing to test other formats across different platforms and audiences.
- Consider expanding retargeting campaigns, which were associated with substantially higher ROAS than non-retargeting campaigns.
- Monitor ROAS trends over time and investigate periods of declining performance before reallocating campaign budgets.
